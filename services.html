02 — How an engagement starts

How an engagement starts

The same way every time.

  1. 01 Step one

    A conversation about the number you care about

    Not traffic, unless traffic is genuinely the KPI. Usually it is installs, orders, revenue, or qualified leads. I want to know which one, and what it is now.

  2. 02 Step two

    An audit before any recommendation

    I establish the current state and whether the reporting behind it can be trusted. Sometimes this is the whole engagement, and sometimes it changes what the engagement should have been.

  3. 03 Step three

    A prioritised plan with the mechanism written down

    What gets changed, in what order, why that order, and what should move as a result. If I cannot say what a task is supposed to move, it does not go on the list.

  4. 04 Step four

    Delivery and reporting against the original number

    Including when it goes the wrong way.
