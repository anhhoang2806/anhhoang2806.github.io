  • 07 Health and supplements · Freelance · measured Dec 2023 – Apr 2025

    Tomorrow’s Nutrition — organic search where the product name outpulls the company name

    741K

    Tomorrow’s Nutrition · organic impressions, 22 Dec 2023 – 21 Apr 2025

    Organic search for a health supplements brand whose flagship product brand is Sunfiber. Sixteen months of Search Console data, and an average position that says the job is unfinished. No before-and-after comparison was pulled, so the case study claims no lift. Sunfiber queries outpull the company name roughly five to one.

    741K impressions, 22.3K clicks · Sunfiber, the product brand, outpulls the company brand · average position 11.1

    Read the Tomorrow’s Nutrition case study

  • 08 Paid media / E-commerce · Jun 2024 – Dec 2025

    Craft Hub — Meta paid media on a licensed-IP craft catalogue

    $8.52M

    Craft Hub · Meta spend, two ad accounts, 2022–2025

    Buying media for a craft brand selling under other people’s licences, across two Meta ad accounts from June 2024 to December 2025, on accounts that had been running since June 2022. The case study reports structure and spread, because the account does not support one blended return and I did not build one. Execution ran with a team.

    $8,522,696.82 account-lifetime Meta spend across two accounts · licensed IP: LOTR, Wicked, Jurassic World, DC · 421 campaigns and ad sets
