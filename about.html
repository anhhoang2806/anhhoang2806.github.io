01 — How I work

How I work

Four things describe most of what I actually do.

  • 01 Diagnosis

    I find the problem before I propose the fix

    Most engagements start with something that is already broken and not visible — a measurement error, a migration that quietly lost a template, a subdomain that stopped serving. I would rather spend the first two weeks establishing what is true than shipping a content calendar.

  • 02 Infrastructure

    I build the infrastructure, not just the pages

    Site architecture, internal linking, schema and performance are the parts that compound. Content sitting on a site that fails Core Web Vitals on 95% of its pages is content you paid for twice.

  • 03 Tooling

    I write code where it is faster than asking for it

    Python, SQL, HTML, CSS and JavaScript, plus Apps Script, Zapier and n8n. It means I can build the vector database, ship the schema, and pull the BigQuery cut myself instead of queuing behind a sprint.

  • 04 Reporting

    I report the number even when it is worse than the last one

    This is the one I would most want a manager to check with a reference. At ELSA I corrected our own reported SEO installs down by roughly 10x because the methodology behind them was wrong.
