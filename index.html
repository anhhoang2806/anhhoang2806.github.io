05 — Method note

Correction · not a growth figure

I corrected my own numbers down by 10x

At ELSA Speak I inherited a web-to-app attribution setup that reported SEO installs at roughly ten times their real volume. The cause was a methodology issue in how AppsFlyer was attributing the web-to-app journey, not a tracking outage — everything looked healthy.

I found it, proved it, and corrected the reported figure downward. That erased a large share of the organic installs my own team was credited with.

I did it because a growth number that is wrong is worse than no number at all. Budget gets allocated against it. Roadmaps get built on it. The same audit surfaced a second problem: a domain-exclusion bug that was silently blocking Smart Banners on regional subdomains. I re-architected the setup to one AppsFlyer workspace per subdomain and the banners started serving.
